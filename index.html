    ◆
    Research Methodology

    Replication and extension of journal-grade LSTM stock prediction. Four deep learning architectures (LSTM, GRU, BiLSTM, LSTM+Attention) are trained across 22 KomVar (variable combinations) using 5-fold walk-forward cross-validation on 2019–2024 training data.

    ◆
    Feature Engineering

    12 technical indicators (SMA, WMA, EMA, MOM, Stochastic, RSI, MACD, ADO, CCI, Williams%R, MFI), fundamental macro variables (IHSG, JISDOR exchange rate, Bank Indonesia rate), and IndoBERT-derived sentiment features including Bullishness Index, Weighted Sentiment, EWMA Momentum, and Dispersion.

    ◆
    Genetic Algorithm Feature Selection

    A genetic algorithm with Ridge regression proxy fitness was run for 30 generations on a population of 20 to select the optimal subset of technical indicators. The GA chromosome encodes binary feature inclusion, minimising validation RMSE via tournament selection, single-point crossover, and random mutation.

    ◆
    Hyperparameter Optimisation

    Grid search across units (100/150/200), dropout (0.1/0.3), lookback timestamps (10/20), and learning rates was followed by Bayesian TPE optimisation via Optuna (50 trials), targeting minimum walk-forward validation RMSE on the two best-performing KomVar configurations.

    Bullishness Index
    ln((1+pos)/(1+neg)) • Filters neutral noise
    Bullishness Index Formula
    Bullishnesst = ln ( (1 + Countpos,t) / (1 + Countneg,t) )

    The logarithmic Bullishness Index focuses on the ratio of positive to negative tweets, filtering the majority of neutral tweets while highlighting days with strong directional consensus. A positive value signals bullish Twitter activity; negative signals bearish activity. Adding 1 prevents division by zero and gracefully handles zero-count days.

    Engagement-Weighted Sentiment
    Weightedt = ∑i [(prob_posi - prob_negi) × (Likesi + Retweetsi + 1)]  /  ∑i (Likesi + Retweetsi + 1)

    High-engagement tweets (viral, many likes and retweets) carry proportionally more weight under the hypothesis that influential content has stronger market impact. The continuous sentiment score (prob_pos - prob_neg) per tweet provides fine-grained signal beyond binary labels.

    Trading Strategy Logic

    The AI-guided strategy generates a buy signal at time t if the LSTM model predicts a higher closing price at t+1 than the current actual close at t. The strategy enters a long (buy) position at open of t+1 and exits if the next prediction is bearish. This simulates a next-day directional trading strategy with no short-selling, no leverage, and no transaction costs. Sharpe ratio is computed with annualised excess returns (252 trading days, BI risk-free rate).

    Diebold-Mariano Test

    The Diebold-Mariano (DM) test formally assesses whether the difference in predictive accuracy between two competing forecasts is statistically significant rather than random. The test statistic is computed as the standardised mean of the loss differential series d_t = L(e1_t) - L(e2_t), where L is either MSE or MAE loss. A p-value below 0.05 rejects the null of equal predictive accuracy. The long-run variance accounts for potential autocorrelation in the loss differential series up to the forecast horizon h.

    Ljung-Box Q-Test

    The Ljung-Box test checks whether the LSTM residuals e_t = y_t - y_hat_t exhibit any remaining autocorrelation up to a specified lag m. The Q statistic follows a chi-squared distribution under the null of white noise. Failing to reject H0 (p > 0.05) confirms that the model has adequately captured the serial structure in the data, and the prediction errors are i.i.d. noise, which is the desired property of a well-specified forecasting model.

    Multi-Step Auto-Regressive Forecasting

    The 5-day forecast is generated by feeding each prediction back as the next input: the model uses the last "timestamps" window of scaled features to predict t+1, then shifts the window by replacing the oldest observation with a synthetic row whose close column equals the predicted value (all other features carried forward). This auto-regressive approach accumulates error over longer horizons but provides a realistic deployment-ready inference pipeline. The 3% confidence band reflects approximate uncertainty based on the model's test-period MAPE.

    How the Simulation Works

    This simulator applies your chosen parameters to the actual LSTM model backtest data from the test period (2025–2026). The AI Signal strategy uses predicted vs actual close prices to generate long signals. Transaction costs are deducted on each trade entry/exit. The confidence threshold filters out low-conviction signals (small predicted moves). Position sizing applies fractional capital allocation. Stop-loss exits positions when intraday loss exceeds the threshold. All metrics are computed from real data — not a simulation of data.
